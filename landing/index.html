Relationship snapping for Blender transforms

Magnets is a free, open-source add-on. While you move, rotate or scale with G R S, it draws guides for nearby alignments, gaps, midpoints and other relationships, then applies the snap when you release.

How a snap happens

Magnets does not replace Blender's transform. It reads the transform in progress, draws the relationships it finds, and applies one snap on release.

A faint guide fades in as the selection nears a relationship.

  • Passive guideA nearby relationship. Neutral color, fades with distance.
  • Engaged guideWill snap. Alignment uses the axis color (X red, Y green, Z blue); other types use the Active Color, magenta by default.
  • YLabelNames the relationship: an axis, a symbol such as ∥ or ◇, or a length. Search any symbol in the Reference.
  • Snap anchor dotThe two points being matched.
  • Landing outlineWhere the selection goes on release.

Guide types

Magnets detects eleven kinds of relationships. Each can be turned on or off in Sidebar ▸ Magnets ▸ Guide Types. The label is the text shown next to the guide in the viewport.

Alignment

X Y Z

Matches one coordinate: origin, pivot, center, face center or bounding-box corner. The label is the axis, plus the remaining distance when it is not zero.

Equal Spacing

⇔ 2 m

Repeats a gap to extend an even row, or centers an object between two neighbors. Measures centers, edges or both (Even Spacing setting).

Equal Size

▭ X · 2 m

While scaling, matches the size of a nearby object along one axis.

Midpoint

◇

Places a point halfway between two others.

Tangency

◎ · ↔ surf

Brings a surface into contact with another, or to a set offset from it: ◎ for curved contact, ↔ surf for a surface offset.

Parallel

∥

Rotates an edge or direction to run alongside another.

Perpendicular

⊥

Rotates an edge or direction to meet another at a right angle.

Collinear

-

Places a point on the line that continues an edge.

Coplanar

▭

Places a point on the plane of a face.

Concentric

◎

Gives a circle or ring the same center as another.

Symmetry

⇔ YZ

Mirrors a point across a world plane (XY, XZ or YZ) through its midpoint with another point.

Rotation and scale

Rotation snaps to the nearest Angle Snap increment (15° by default) when you release close to one. Scale matches a nearby object's size. Both preview the result and print a note at the pivot, for example → 45° or = Cube.002 · 2 m. No note means no snap.

Snapping modes

By default Blender's own G R S stay in charge and Magnets snaps once on release. Precision Mode replaces them with Magnets' operators, which hold the snap while you drag.

Selection follows the pointer

While dragging
Guides only. The selection follows the pointer.
On release
One snap to the engaged guides.
Undo
One step in Object Mode, two in Edit Mode.
Blender features
All available: numeric input, orbiting, proportional editing.
While dragging
Locks onto a guide and holds it until the pointer passes Break Distance.
Supported
X Y Z axis locks and mouse-wheel zoom.
Not supported
Numeric input, orbiting mid-drag, proportional editing.
Edit Mode
Rotate and scale fall back to Blender's transform.

Enable in Sidebar ▸ Magnets ▸ Precision Mode or in the add-on preferences.

Blender's Snap toggle (magnet icon)

Off: Magnets snaps as described above.

Reference

Scene options are in 3D Viewport ▸ Sidebar (N) ▸ Magnets. Colors, indicators and the shortcut are in Edit ▸ Preferences ▸ Add-ons ▸ Magnets. The filter also covers viewport labels, so you can search for a symbol such as ◎.

Tolerances

Distances are measured on screen, between the pointer and a guide, in pixels that follow Blender's Resolution Scale. Presets set them together.

Installation

No additional dependencies.

  1. Download the zip

    Get magnets-<version>.zip from the releases page. Keep it zipped.

  2. Install from disk

    Open Edit ▸ Preferences ▸ Get Extensions, use the menu at the top right, choose Install from Disk and select the zip.

  3. Try it

    Press G and move an object toward another one. Guides appear as you approach. Release to snap.

Troubleshooting

  • No guides at all. Magnets may be off: check the header button or press Shift Alt M. Also check that Passive Guides is on.
  • Guides show but nothing snaps. Snap to Guides is off. It is the first option in the panel.
  • Blender's magnet icon is on. Magnets stands aside while Blender snapping is active. Turn it off, or turn off Yield to Blender Snapping.
  • You pressed Esc or held Ctrl. Both skip the snap on release, by design.
  • Something else. Turn on Debug Logging in the preferences, then open an issue with the console output.
